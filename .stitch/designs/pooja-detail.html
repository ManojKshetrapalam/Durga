Frequently Asked Questions

Can elderly family members sit on chairs during the Homa? expand_more
Yes, comfortable wooden and padded chairs are specially arranged inside the Yagashala for elderly devotees and anyone unable to sit on traditional floor mats.
Will prasada be sent if we cannot attend in person? expand_more
Yes, e-Homa sankalpa can be booked online and sanctified prasada, holy raksha thread, and kumkuma will be dispatched via Indian Speed Post with SMS tracking within 48 hours.
Can we offer special fruits or personal flowers? expand_more
Yes, devotees are welcome to bring fresh local flowers, dry fruits, or tulasi and hand them directly to the chief Vedic priest prior to the Poornahuti offering.

Devotee Care & Sanctity

All homas adhere strictly to Vedic Agama Shastra under the guidance of hereditary temple Archakas.
